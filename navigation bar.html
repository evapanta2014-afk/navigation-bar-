Sefton High School

Sefton High School is a government-funded, co-educational, partially academically selective and comprehensive secondary school located at 41-43 Hector Street, Sefton, NSW, 2162. Sefton was established: 1961 (became NSW's first partially selective school in 1989). It has about 1100 students. And the schools motto is Sincerity, Scholarship, Service.
